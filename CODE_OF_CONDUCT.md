# Code Of Conduct

We want Skills Layer CLI to be a respectful, practical, and welcoming project for users and contributors.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while being precise about risks and tradeoffs.
- Keep security reports private until they are resolved or responsibly disclosed.
- Avoid harassment, insults, discrimination, or repeated disruptive behavior.

## Scope

This applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and related project communication.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and may restrict participation when behavior harms users, contributors, or project safety.
